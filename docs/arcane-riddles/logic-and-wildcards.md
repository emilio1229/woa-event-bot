# Logic, Wordplay & Arcane Wildcards — Expansion Drafts

**Drafts only. A few are flagged for revision because the answer is too broad.**

## LOG-001 — The More You Take
**Question:** The more steps you take, the more of me you leave behind. What am I?
**Answer:** Footsteps. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## LOG-002 — The Room With No Doors
**Question:** I have keys but no locks, space but no room, and an enter key but no doorway. What am I?
**Answer:** A keyboard. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## LOG-003 — The Broken Promise
**Question:** Say my name and I disappear. What am I?
**Answer:** Silence. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## LOG-004 — The More It Dries
**Question:** I grow wetter while doing the job of making something else dry. What am I?
**Answer:** A towel. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## LOG-005 — The One-Way Door
**Question:** I can be entered, but I cannot be walked through; I can be solved, but I am not a puzzle. What am I?
**Answer:** “A problem” is not a clean fit — revise before approval.

## LOG-006 — The Unseen Weight
**Question:** You can carry me for years without putting me in a bag. Share me, and I may become lighter; hide me, and I may become heavier. What am I?
**Answer:** Could be worry or a secret — choose one and rewrite.

## LOG-007 — The Candle's Gift
**Question:** The more I give away, the smaller I become, and the light I offer is born from my own change. What am I?
**Answer:** A candle. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## LOG-008 — The Arcane Calendar
**Question:** I return again and again, but never bring the same day twice. I can mark a festival, a season, or a promised challenge. What am I?
**Answer:** Ambiguous between a calendar and a cycle — tighten before approval.

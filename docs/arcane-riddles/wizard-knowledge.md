## WZ-001 — The Three Arts
**Question:** One studies the hidden rules, one draws power through a pact, and one follows a craft older than the halls. Together, their names stand beneath a magical community's banner. What three callings are described?
**Answer:** Wizard, warlock, and witch (also accept “Wizards, Warlocks & Witches”).
**Difficulty:** Easy · **Note:** Similar to existing WoA motto lore; consider replacing with a more distinct question.

## WZ-002 — The Wand's Work
**Question:** I have no mind, yet in a practiced hand I become an extension of intent. In stories I may focus a spell, but I cannot supply the wisdom to choose it. What am I?
**Answer:** A wand. · **Difficulty:** Easy

## WZ-003 — The Circle Without Walls
**Question:** It may be drawn in chalk, marked by candles, or imagined in a ritual. Its boundary is meaningful even when no stone is raised. What is it?
**Answer:** A magic circle / ritual circle. · **Difficulty:** Medium

**Answer:** A prophecy. · **Difficulty:** Easy

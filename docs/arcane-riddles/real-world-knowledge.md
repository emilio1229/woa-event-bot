# Real-World Knowledge — Expansion Drafts

**Drafts only — factual answers should be checked before approval.**

## IRL-001 — The Red Planet
**Question:** I am named for a Roman god, look rusty from afar, and host the tallest known volcano in the Solar System. Which planet am I?
**Answer:** Mars. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Space

## IRL-002 — The Borrowed Light
**Question:** I appear to shine at night, but I make no visible light of my own. My changing face is a dance of sunlight and shadow. What am I?
**Answer:** The Moon. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Space

## IRL-003 — The Metal That Floats
**Question:** Most metals sink in water, but one familiar silvery metal is less dense than water and can float on it. Name the metal.
**Answer:** Lithium. **Knowledge-only:** do not attempt an experiment; lithium reacts strongly with water. · **Difficulty:** Hard · **Category:** Science

## IRL-004 — The Library of Life
**Question:** I am a molecule that carries inherited instructions in nearly every living thing. My familiar shape is a twisted ladder. What am I?
**Answer:** DNA. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Biology

## IRL-005 — The Mapmaker's Line
**Question:** I circle Earth halfway between the North and South Poles, dividing the planet into northern and southern halves. What am I?
**Answer:** The Equator. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Geography

## IRL-006 — The Timekeeper's Extra Day
**Question:** Most years have 365 days, but a calendar sometimes adds one day to keep its seasons aligned with Earth's trip around the Sun. What kind of year is it?
**Answer:** A leap year. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Everyday science

## IRL-007 — The Soundless Shadow
**Question:** I travel faster than sound, so you may see me before you hear the event that made me. What natural flash am I?
**Answer:** Lightning. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Weather

## IRL-008 — The Desert's Secret
**Question:** A cactus is famous for surviving dry places, but not every desert is hot. What broad climate feature defines a desert?
**Answer:** Very low precipitation / very little rainfall. · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Category:** Earth science

## IRL-009 — The Giant's Footprint
**Question:** I am not a planet, but I am the largest animal known to have lived on Earth. My heart and body belong to the ocean. What am I?
**Answer:** The blue whale. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Nature

## IRL-010 — The Invisible Ocean
**Question:** I surround Earth, protect life, and press against you even though you rarely feel me. I am a mixture of gases. What am I?
**Answer:** The atmosphere. · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Category:** Earth science
